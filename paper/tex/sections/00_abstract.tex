Small language models in the 1B--7B range are attractive for on-device agent deployment but routinely fail at structured tool use. We present \textbf{A.L.F.R.E.D.}, a middleware that compiles successful agent trajectories into deterministic execution patterns and routes user intents across two tiers: small-LLM Reflexer, and full-LLM Thinker fallback. On a 53-task benchmark spanning calendar, email, and messaging skills with ministral-3-3b, A.L.F.R.E.D.\ achieves \textbf{96.2\%} router accuracy and \textbf{91.4\%} atomic gold-command match. We report three deployment findings: success-rate stats poisoning, sibling-pattern hijacking, and a \textbf{9.4\,pp} accuracy gain from position-weighted scoring.
While maintaining 95\% of success rate from bigger LLMs.
