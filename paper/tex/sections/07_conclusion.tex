% Conclusion - v0 draft.
A.L.F.R.E.D.\ shows that personal-assistant agents do not need a large language model on every invocation. By compiling successful trajectories into deterministic patterns and routing across two tiers (a small-LLM Reflexer and a full-LLM Thinker fallback), the system reaches 96.2\% router accuracy and 91.4\% atomic gold-command match on a 53-task benchmark with a 3B local model, while running the routed path roughly an order of magnitude faster than the equivalent ReAct loop and preserving about 95\% of the larger model's success rate. The three deployment findings reported here, namely success-rate stats poisoning, sibling-pattern hijacking, and the dominance of position-weighted scoring, are concrete artefacts of moving small-model routing from prototype to repeatable benchmark. Future work centres on online stats feedback to replace the offline distiller loop, a learned semantic router for off-distribution paraphrases, and trace-based distillation. Source code and the benchmark harness are released open-source.
